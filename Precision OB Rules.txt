Methodology & caveats
- Signal: the first precision order block born on/after the 26W breakout session, per breakout cycle - the live scanner's own first_zone_after_breakout rule, applied to every historical cycle. Zones come from ob_precision.replay(), the Pine-exact port
the live scanner runs, with config.yaml defaults: zone = the origin candle's open-to-low, origin = the nearest bearish or neutral candle within 8 bars before a displacement (close > 8-bar structure, rvol >= 1.8, range >= 1.2 ATR, body >= 55%,
close-location-value >= 0.72).
- Entry A is the origin candle's close - the ask. It uses one bar of hindsight: the origin only becomes a precision OB when the displacement bar after it closes. Entry B (displacement close) is reported beside it everywhere; prefer B when deciding
what is actually tradable.
- The OB candle must sit on/after the breakout session and close above the 26W level. Zones born on the breakout session whose origin candle predates it (the breakout bar itself is the displacement) are counted in the funnel and excluded - the
SMSPHARMA shape.
- Target = the highest high printed between the breakout session and the entry session (sell limit); stop = the 26W breakout level (stop order); 90-session time stop. Fills are gap-aware: through the target fills at the open (better), through the stop at
the open (worse); a session touching both resolves by its open, else counts conservatively as a loss.
- 26W breakout cycles are dated from the candle-derived c02 cross (derive_26w_breakout semantics) with the 26-week cross-lock. The live waiting list adds the c03..c13 weekly momentum gate, so this sample is a superset of the live feed.
- Data: eod2 (github.com/BennyThadikaran/eod2_data) NSE daily OHLCV, adjusted for splits and bonuses, through 2026-09-25. universe.csv is today's universe: 2021-era delistings are absent (survivorship).
- In-sample and descriptive: the configuration was chosen from this same history (it is the accepted Tap 1 configuration with an earlier entry). No portfolio simulation - overlapping signals count as independent trades; capital limits, correlation and
slippage beyond the flat 0.22% round trip are not modelled.
- The 26W level as stop is order-based (any low at/below the level stops the trade); the live zone invalidation is close-based and looser.
